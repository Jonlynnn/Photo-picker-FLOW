Privacy Policy

Your photographs belong to you. Here is how FLOW handles data.

Updated September 16, 2026 · Applies to the FLOW macOS app and this website
01 Local processing02 File access03 Retention04 Exports05 Website06 Contact

01 · Processed locally, not uploaded

FLOW is a local photo-culling and magazine-making app. The current version does not require an account, does not offer FLOW cloud sync, and does not upload photographs, culling records, or keyboard settings to a FLOW server. It does not include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

To provide its features, FLOW processes photographs you authorize and related metadata on your device. This can include filenames, file paths, dimensions, capture dates, and camera information. Sessions, keep and discard decisions, progress, magazine settings, and preferences are stored locally.

FLOW stores local cover thumbnails to make the session list faster and may create local preview caches. Local processing therefore does not mean that no image data is ever stored. Culling decisions do not modify original photographs.

The keyboard selection feature reads the minimum device-identification information needed to distinguish built-in and external keyboards and save a configuration locally. Culling shortcuts are not used to collect or upload what you type.

02 · File access is granted by you

You select files or folders through the macOS file picker. FLOW stores the corresponding access authorization so that you can continue a session later. You choose the destination whenever you export.

You can manage stored access in Settings → Privacy & Data → Authorized Locations. Removing authorization does not delete original files, but FLOW may ask for access again when you reopen a related session. You can also manage system permissions in macOS settings.

03 · Retention and deletion

Local sessions and settings remain available so you can continue later and are generally retained until you remove them. FLOW may also keep local recovery data. Clearing cover thumbnails and clearing all local app data are separate actions; read the confirmation shown by the app before proceeding.

Deleting a session, thumbnail, or FLOW local data does not delete imported original photographs. Exported files must be managed separately at their destination. Copies held by system backups or third-party synchronization services are outside FLOW's control.

04 · Exports are initiated by you

You decide when to export selected photographs or a magazine and where those files are saved. FLOW does not automatically publish content to social platforms.

If you choose iCloud Drive, a network volume, or another synchronized folder, that provider may process the exported files under its own policy. Exported photographs may retain original metadata, including location data where present. Review files before sharing them publicly.

05 · Website and app data are separate

This version of the website does not embed analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. Its illustrations are served with the site rather than loaded from third-party image servers.

The website stores only your light or dark theme preference in the current browser's localStorage. You can remove it by clearing site data, and the website remains usable if local storage is disabled.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address, request time, path, and browser information to deliver and secure the site. That processing is governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy documentation. FLOW does not receive an in-app analytics profile from this hosting activity.
