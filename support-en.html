System requirements

FLOW requires macOS 26 or later. RAW preview availability depends on the formats supported by your version of macOS.

Privacy and local data

FLOW processes photographs locally and stores sessions and preferences on your Mac.

Read the Privacy Policy ↗

Before contacting support

Please include your FLOW version, macOS version, Mac model, and a short description of what happened. Do not attach original photographs unless they are essential and you are comfortable sharing them.

Contact

A dedicated public support email address will be added before launch. This page must be updated before it is submitted as the App Store Support URL.
